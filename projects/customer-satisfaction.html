Customer Satisfaction Prediction

Predictive analytics using structured and unstructured customer-support ticket data.

PythonMachine LearningCustomer Analytics
View GitHub Repository ↗← Back to Projects

Project Overview

Predicts customer satisfaction ratings using customer-support information and demonstrates a practical workflow for customer-experience analytics.

Business Problem

Customer satisfaction is an important indicator of service quality. Predictive analysis can help identify patterns associated with lower satisfaction.

Data Approach

The project combines structured information with customer-support ticket content and applies preprocessing and feature engineering.

Modeling

Machine-learning models are evaluated to identify useful predictive patterns and support interpretation of customer experience drivers.

Business Value

The analysis can help organizations prioritize service improvements and identify areas where customer experience may require attention.
